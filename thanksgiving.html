About Thanksgiving

The fourth Thursday of November in the United States.

Also known as: Thanksgiving Day, Turkey Day

Thanksgiving in the United States is a federal holiday held on the fourth Thursday of November. It has been fixed to that Thursday by law since 1941.

It is traditionally a day for family gatherings and a large meal centered on roast turkey. The long weekend also marks the start of the holiday shopping season, with Black Friday the following day.

Traditions

  • Turkey dinner with family
  • The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York
  • NFL football games
  • Sharing what you are thankful for

Thanksgiving Around the World

United States

A federal holiday on the fourth Thursday of November. The next day is Black Friday, the traditional start of holiday shopping.

Canada

Canadian Thanksgiving is earlier, on the second Monday of October, and is a statutory holiday in most provinces.

Liberia

Liberia marks its own Thanksgiving Day on the first Thursday of November.

Germany

Erntedankfest, a harvest thanksgiving festival, is held in churches on the first Sunday of October.
