About Valentine's Day

February 14, the day for love and friendship.

Also known as: Saint Valentine's Day, Valentines Day, Feast of Saint Valentine

Valentine's Day is celebrated on February 14 in many countries around the world. It is named after Saint Valentine and has been linked with romantic love since the Middle Ages.

Couples exchange cards, flowers and chocolates, and many people celebrate friendship too. In Japan and South Korea, White Day on March 14 is the return-gift day.

Origins & communities

Historical roots
Valentine's Day combines traditions associated with Christian martyrs named Valentine and the later idea of romantic love that developed in medieval Europe.
Communities & traditions
It is now mainly a secular celebration of love and friendship in many countries, with locally adapted customs in Europe, the Americas, Asia and beyond.

Traditions

  • Valentine cards and love notes
  • Roses and chocolates
  • Romantic dinners
  • Galentine's celebrations with friends

Valentine's Day Around the World

These examples cover major regional and diaspora traditions. They are representative, not an exhaustive list of every country or community that observes Valentine's Day; customs can also vary within a country.

United States, United Kingdom, Canada & Australia

Couples exchange cards, flowers and chocolates, and restaurants book up weeks in advance.

Japan & South Korea

Women traditionally give chocolate on February 14, and men return the gesture on White Day, March 14.

India & South Asia

Urban celebrations often include cards, flowers and a commercial Valentine's Week, while participation and attitudes vary widely by community.

Finland & Estonia

February 14 is commonly framed as a day of friendship as well as romance, with cards and small gifts exchanged among friends.

Philippines

Flowers, gifts and dinners are common, and some cities or organizations hold large community or civil wedding ceremonies around February 14.

Latin America

Many countries celebrate love and friendship on February 14, sometimes extending the emphasis beyond couples to friends and family.

Brazil

Brazil's main romantic celebration is Dia dos Namorados on June 12, so this February 14 countdown does not represent the country's principal local date.
