About Christmas

December 25, wherever you are in the world.

Also known as: Xmas, Christmas Day, Noel

Christmas is celebrated on December 25 by billions of people around the world. It marks the birth of Jesus for Christians and has also become a widely shared cultural holiday.

Because Christmas starts at midnight local time, it arrives first in Kiribati and New Zealand and last in American Samoa. This countdown uses your own time zone by default, and you can switch to another city.

Origins & communities

Historical roots
Christmas began as an early Christian commemoration of the birth of Jesus. December 25 became established in the Western church by the fourth century.
Communities & traditions
It is observed by Christians across many denominations and countries, and by many non-Christians as a cultural or family holiday. Some Orthodox churches celebrate on a different civil date.

Traditions

  • Decorating the Christmas tree
  • Exchanging gifts
  • Christmas Eve dinner and midnight Mass
  • Carols and Santa Claus

Christmas Around the World

These examples cover major regional and diaspora traditions. They are representative, not an exhaustive list of every country or community that observes Christmas; customs can also vary within a country.

United States & Canada

Christmas Day is a public holiday. Many families open gifts on the morning of December 25.

United Kingdom & Ireland

Christmas Day and Boxing Day (December 26) are public holidays, with a roast dinner and Christmas pudding.

Australia & New Zealand

Christmas falls in summer, so beach days and barbecues are common. Boxing Day is a public holiday too.

Philippines

One of the longest Christmas seasons in the world. Simbang Gabi dawn Masses run from December 16 to 24.

Germany, Austria & Central Europe

Christmas markets fill Advent, while many families exchange gifts on Christmas Eve and observe December 25–26 as holidays.

Nordic countries

Christmas Eve is usually the main family celebration, with seasonal meals, candles and gift-giving; Finland also has its Christmas sauna and declaration of Christmas peace.

Mexico, Central America & much of South America

Catholic traditions include Advent gatherings, nativity scenes, Christmas Eve Mass and a late Nochebuena family meal; local foods and festivities vary widely.

Spain, Portugal & Italy

Nativity scenes, Christmas Eve or Christmas Day meals and church services are central; gift-giving may also extend to Epiphany in January.

Eastern Europe & Orthodox communities

Traditions include fasting, liturgy and family meals. Churches using older liturgical calendars may observe Christmas on January 7 rather than December 25.

Ethiopia, Eritrea & Egypt's Coptic community

Many Orthodox Christians celebrate after a period of fasting, commonly on January 7, with long church services and distinctive local meals.

Middle Eastern Christian communities

Christians in Lebanon, Jordan, Palestine, Syria, Iraq and neighboring countries combine church liturgy, family meals, visits and local sweets, despite differing calendars and circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days until Christmas?

When is Christmas 2027?

Is Christmas always on December 25?

Yes for most of the world. Orthodox churches that follow the Julian calendar, such as in Russia and Serbia, celebrate on January 7.

Which country celebrates Christmas first?

Kiribati's Line Islands (UTC+14) reach midnight first, followed by New Zealand. American Samoa and Baker Island are among the last.
