About Christmas

December 25, wherever you are in the world.

Also known as: Xmas, Christmas Day, Noel

Christmas is celebrated on December 25 by billions of people around the world. It marks the birth of Jesus for Christians and has also become a widely shared cultural holiday.

Because Christmas starts at midnight local time, it arrives first in Kiribati and New Zealand and last in American Samoa. This countdown uses your own time zone by default, and you can switch to another city.

Traditions

  • Decorating the Christmas tree
  • Exchanging gifts
  • Christmas Eve dinner and midnight Mass
  • Carols and Santa Claus

Christmas Around the World

United States & Canada

Christmas Day is a public holiday. Many families open gifts on the morning of December 25.

United Kingdom & Ireland

Christmas Day and Boxing Day (December 26) are public holidays, with a roast dinner and Christmas pudding.

Australia & New Zealand

Christmas falls in summer, so beach days and barbecues are common. Boxing Day is a public holiday too.

Philippines

One of the longest Christmas seasons in the world. Simbang Gabi dawn Masses run from December 16 to 24.

Germany

The main celebration is on Christmas Eve (Heiligabend), and Christmas markets open throughout Advent.

Is Christmas always on December 25?

Yes for most of the world. Orthodox churches that follow the Julian calendar, such as in Russia and Serbia, celebrate on January 7.

Which country celebrates Christmas first?

Kiribati's Line Islands (UTC+14) reach midnight first, followed by New Zealand. American Samoa and Baker Island are among the last.
