Đọc bằng tiếng Việt

When you contact us

Tell us what happened and include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot may help. For a purchase issue, include the store order ID if available. Never send a password, one-time code, or full payment card details.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel an active subscription in your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Deleting the app or your Sắp Cưới account does not cancel a store subscription. For a refund, submit a request to Apple or Google Play.

Account and privacy

See the account deletion instructions to delete an account or individual records. Read the Sắp Cưới Privacy Policy for data practices. Email us if you cannot access the app or need help with your data.

About the app

Sắp Cưới helps couples track milestones, prepare a wedding checklist, manage a budget and guest list, and save memories. Learn more on the Sắp Cưới page.
