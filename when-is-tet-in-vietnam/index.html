Is Tet a public holiday in Vietnam?

Yes. For 2027, Vietnam's announced schedule gives civil servants and public employees seven consecutive days off, February 4–10, including five Tet holiday days and two compensatory days. Private employers may select one of the announced five-day arrangements and must notify employees at least 30 days before the break. Your own days off depend on your employer's schedule.

Sources: Vietnam Government Portal (English) and Báo Điện tử Chính phủ (30-day notice).

Frequently asked questions

When is Tet in Vietnam in 2027?

The first day of Tet 2027 is Saturday, February 6, 2027. It begins at midnight in Vietnam (UTC+7).

When is Vietnamese New Year's Eve in 2027?

New Year's Eve is Friday, February 5, 2027, the 29th and final day of the lunar year. The new lunar year begins at 00:00 on Saturday, February 6, 2027, Vietnam time.

Is Tet the same as Chinese New Year?

Tet is Vietnam's Lunar New Year, with its own customs, foods and calendar traditions. The dates often coincide, but the celebrations are culturally distinct.

How long is the official Tet 2027 break?

Public-sector staff have February 4–10 off. Businesses choose from the announced five-day options; check the schedule set by your employer.
