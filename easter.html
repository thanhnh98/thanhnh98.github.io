About Easter

Easter Sunday, the most important Christian feast.

Also known as: Easter Sunday, Resurrection Sunday, Pascha

Easter celebrates the resurrection of Jesus and is the most important feast in the Christian calendar. It falls on the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after March 21, so the date moves between March 22 and April 25.

Easter Sunday ends Holy Week and the 40 days of Lent. Alongside church services, many families decorate eggs, hold egg hunts and share a festive meal.

Origins & communities

Historical roots
Easter arose among the earliest Christian communities as the commemoration of the resurrection of Jesus, in the historical and calendrical setting of Jewish Passover.
Communities & traditions
It is observed by Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox and other Christian communities worldwide. Western and Eastern churches may celebrate on different dates because they use different calendar calculations.

Traditions

  • Easter Sunday church services
  • Decorating and hunting eggs
  • Chocolate eggs and the Easter Bunny
  • Family lunch

Easter Around the World

These examples cover major regional and diaspora traditions. They are representative, not an exhaustive list of every country or community that observes Easter; customs can also vary within a country.

United States & Canada

Egg hunts are a big tradition, including the White House Easter Egg Roll on Easter Monday.

United Kingdom, Ireland & Australia

Good Friday is a public holiday, and in most regions Easter Monday is too, making a four-day weekend.

Philippines

Holy Week (Semana Santa) is observed nationwide, and Maundy Thursday and Good Friday are public holidays.

Germany & Central Europe

Painted eggs, Easter nests, bonfires and decorated branches are common, with regional variations across Germany, Austria, Switzerland and neighboring countries.

Spain, Portugal, Italy & Latin America

Holy Week processions, Passion observances, church services and family foods are central; customs vary greatly between cities and countries.

Greece, Cyprus, Romania, Serbia, Russia & other Eastern Christian communities

Midnight liturgy, candles, greetings of the Resurrection and festive foods are common. A different Paschal calculation often places Orthodox Easter on another date.

Ethiopia, Eritrea & Egypt's Coptic community

Easter follows a long fast and is welcomed with extended liturgy, family meals and local breads or meat dishes according to each church's calendar.

Middle Eastern Christian communities

Christians in Palestine, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Iraq and neighboring countries observe Holy Week and Easter through liturgy, processions, family visits and regional sweets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days until Easter?

There are 175 days until Easter 2027, which is on Sunday, March 28, 2027. The live countdown on this page updates every second.

When is Easter 2028?

Easter 2028 is on Sunday, April 16, 2028.

Why does Easter change date every year?

Easter is tied to the first full moon after the March equinox, so it can fall on any Sunday between March 22 and April 25.

Is Good Friday a public holiday?

Good Friday, two days before Easter, is a public holiday in many countries including the UK, Australia, Germany and Canada.
